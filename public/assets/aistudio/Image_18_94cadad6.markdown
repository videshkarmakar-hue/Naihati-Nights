## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an austere, architectural elegance rooted in deep obsidian tones and regal deep electric violet. Engineered for discerning software experiences, executive-level creative platforms, and boutique digital ateliers, the aesthetic communicates quiet authority, precision, and permanence.

The visual direction rejects synthetic tropes: no heavy drop-shadows, no diffused chromatic blooms, and no hyper-saturated novelty gradients. Instead, it relies on structural discipline, refined surface stratification, razor-thin structural borders, and purposeful contrast ratios. By anchoring deep noir alongside tactile violet accents and crisp typographic layers, the system instills an atmosphere of confident calm, clarity, and deliberate craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure value contrast and monochromatic depth, illuminated by restrained violet highlights.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#050505` (Noir). Deepest ground level for pure contrast and OLED clarity.
- **Surface Layer 1:** `#0E0E12` (Obsidian Base). Primary card and structural panel surface.
- **Surface Layer 2:** `#16161D` (Obsidian Raised). Elevated states, popovers, and interactive modules.
- **Surface Layer 3:** `#1E1E28` (Obsidian Highlight). Hover states, highlighted rows, and table headers.
- **Primary Brand:** `#3A0CA3` (Regal Electric Violet). Confident, saturated anchor for primary actions, critical state indicators, and focal elements.
- **Secondary Accent:** `#8B5CF6` (Vibrant Iris). Interactive highlights, focused active indicators, and secondary badges.
- **Tertiary Accent:** `#C4B5FD` (Pale Lavender). Delicate micro-borders, focus rings, and soft iconography.
- **Typography:**
  - High-Contrast White (`#F8FAFC`): Primary headers and high-priority data.
  - Soft Zinc (`#94A3B8`): Body copy, labels, and secondary context.
  - Muted Zinc (`#64748B`): Placeholders, disabled states, and metadata timestamps.
- **Borders & Dividers:** High-precision alpha strokes (`rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.08)` default, `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.18)` interactive).

All color pairings meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA legibility benchmarks. Violet fills are reserved strictly for high-intent user interaction, keeping the overall environment tranquil and distraction-free.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes Plus Jakarta Sans exclusively, leaning into its balanced geometry, wide apertures, and contemporary cadence.

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered with tight negative letter tracking to construct solid, editorial statements. The high-contrast white text cuts sharply against dark obsidian backplates.
- **Body & Longform:** Balanced with generous line height and neutral spacing to ensure reading ease without optical eye fatigue during extended usage.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted with slightly increased tracking and medium/semibold weights to anchor form inputs, status chips, and metadata tags with absolute clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an architectural, 12-column responsive grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet devices and a 4-column column system on mobile screens.

- **Baseline System:** A rigorous 4px baseline rhythm governs all components and vertical stacks.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major sections use `space-2xl` (64px) or `space-xl` (40px) to establish breathable, intentional negative space.
- **Component Tightness:** Content within cards, dialogs, and controls relies on precise internal steps (`space-sm` to `space-lg`) to preserve information density without visual crowding.
- **Adaptive Reflow:** Tables collapse into structured key-value list cards on mobile viewports. Dense multidimensional grids simplify into single-column vertical stacks with uniform padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via layered obsidian tones paired with crisp, hairline borders rather than artificial illumination or heavy dropshadows.

1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Canvas substrate (`#050505`).
2. **Structural Surfaces (Level 1):** Primary panels and base cards (`#0E0E12`) bounded by a subtle 1px border (`rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.08)`).
3. **Floating Modules (Level 2):** Context menus, elevated modals, and dropdown overlays (`#16161D`) bounded by an interactive hairline border (`rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.16)`). An ambient, low-spread shadow is applied solely for separation: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
4. **Transient Flyouts & Drawers (Level 3):** Highest layer surfaces (`#1E1E28`) accompanied by an architectural edge outline: `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

No neon edge lighting, colored outer glows, or blurred drop shadows are permitted. Physical surface separation relies entirely on value shifts and geometric delineation.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes a restrained, subtle corner curvature (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius, 8px on large containers) to maintain an architectural, engineered aesthetic.

- **Micro Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Chips):** 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius. Evokes precision instrument design.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, Modals, Panels):** 8px (`0.5rem`) border radius. Softens boundary edges without compromising clean grid geometry.
- **Indicators & Avatars:** Circular (`50%` or pill) forms are reserved strictly for human portraits and unread notification pips to immediately distinguish dynamic assets from static UI scaffolding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in regal electric violet (`#3A0CA3`), text in `#F8FAFC`, subtle inset border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Hover shifts to `#480FB8`. Active state deepens to `#2E0A82`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background in `#0E0E12`, hairline stroke of `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.15)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover shifts background to `#16161D` with border at `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.3)`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent fill, `#94A3B8` text. Hover transitions to `#16161D` and text turns `#F8FAFC`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact height (24px to 28px) with a 4px corner radius.
- Background uses `#16161D` with an ultra-thin 1px border of `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.12)`.
- Primary label in `#94A3B8`. Violet variant employs a solid `#3A0CA3` backing at 15% opacity with `#C4B5FD` typography.

### Input Fields
- Surface background set to `#0E0E12` with a 1px border in `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.1)`.
- Height pinned to 40px, text styled in `#F8FAFC`, placeholder muted to `#64748B`.
- Focused state replaces the default stroke with a crisp, dual-layered hairline ring: 1px `#8B5CF6` border paired with an outer offset ring of 1px at `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 16px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with a 1px hairline border in `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.2)`.
- Inactive surface rests on `#0E0E12`.
- Checked state fills with `#3A0CA3`, framing a razor-sharp `#F8FAFC` checkmark icon or center pip.

### Cards
- Surface background in `#0E0E12` framed with a 1px border of `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.08)`.
- Padding set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Hoverable cards transition border to `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.2)` and elevate background to `#16161D` with an ease-out timing curve (150ms).

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table header row styled in `#0E0E12` with micro-uppercase labels (`label-sm`) in `#64748B`.
- Alternating horizontal rules rendered in `1px solid rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.05)`.
- Row hover triggers a subtle shift to `#16161D` across the entire row width.
